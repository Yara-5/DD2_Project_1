## 2. Quick start
Tools: Yosys 0.52, Icarus Verilog 12.0, Magic 8.3.684, Netgen 1.5.133, ngspice-47 with the
BSIM4 stress fix (course image, sha256 `51123371…`), Python 3.12 with gdsfactory 9.51.0,
klayout 0.30.12 and matplotlib 3.11.2 (`requirements.txt`).

Put machine-specific paths in `local.mk` (see the top of the `Makefile`), then:

    make setup     # links the PDK into Tutorial 3's folder for its tools.sh
    make check     # under a minute
    make all       # about 4 minutes for inv_1 and nand2_1
    make test      # make all, then every broken case

## 3. What the tool does
It reads a cell request (Appendix A of the overview), checks that its expression and truth
table agree, and builds the cell from a stage spec: the transistors, their sizes and one Euler
path per row. `prepare_layout.py` places the gates on the HD frame of `given/plan_hd.json`,
sizes every contact and pitch from `src/rules.json` and routes on li1; `generate_gds.py`
draws that plan with GDSfactory. The cell is then checked: switch level, DRC in tapped rows
with abutment, the implant/npc layer check, LVS, RC-LVS and LVS against the installed HD cell,
the frame comparison with HD, a transistor-level simulation of the RC netlist, and the
Verilog/Liberty model check.

## 5. Layout
- `src/` the code, one script per step, and `rules.json` (the design rules behind every number)
- `tests/requests/`, `tests/specs/` the flow's inputs; `tests/broken/` the broken cases
- `cells/<name>/` every view of one cell (generated by `make all`, not committed)
- `results/<check>/` the check reports (generated by `make all`, not committed)
- `libs/`, `report/` Liberty libraries and the report (empty for now)
- `ai/` the AI use and mistakes logs; `docs/interface/` the Appendix A examples

## 6. Results
These files are not in the repository; `make all` writes them (about 4 minutes).
- `results/pdk_rules/report.json`: all 20 rule values match the installed sky130A.tech
- `results/drc/verification.json`: 48 abutment contexts with 0 DRC errors; layer check passes on both generated and both installed cells
- `results/lvs/verification.json`: LVS, RC-LVS and LVS against `sky130_fd_sc_hd__inv_1`/`nand2_1` match
- `results/hd_comparison/comparison.json`: inv_1 4 sites (HD 3), nand2_1 6 sites (HD 3); frame XOR clean
- `results/transistor_level/`: every DC row and every single-input edge passes
- `results/model_level/`: Verilog and Liberty function match the request; broken models rejected

## 9. Limitations
- Steps 2 and 3 (`synthesize.py`) build a checked transistor network for any request, but
  step 4 (Euler paths) is not written: layout still needs a hand-written spec in
  `tests/specs/`, so only inv_1 and nand2_1 reach GDS. Multi-stage cells, folding, unequal
  finger widths and diffusion breaks are refused by the layout step.
- `synthesize.py` does not share identical sub-stages, and only considers algebraic
  factorings of minimum SOP/POS covers.
- The cells are wider than HD (gate pitch 0.64 um against 0.42 um): each poly contact has its
  own NPC opening, and contacts use the larger enclosure on all four sides.
- No characterization (section 5): the Liberty cell is functional only, there are no SA/SB on
  the extracted transistors, and no response file is written. The round trip of section 3.3,
  the Yosys/ABC `cec` check and the a21o_1/mux2_1 comparison are not done.
- Parasitic and timing comparisons with HD (Table 4) are not done.
